The science behind LOC Analyzer

A rigorous look at the problem, literature, methodology, and technology stack of our research project.

📖

Literature Survey

Existing research demonstrates that assessment moderation benefits from AI-driven analysis and automation. Recent advances in natural language processing and machine learning have enabled scalable, objective evaluation of exam content and marking schemes. LOC Analyzer synthesizes findings from educational data mining, automated grading, and quality assurance to build an evidence-based platform.

RESEARCH GAP

What existing solutions miss

💡
Costly Moderation
Manual moderation is time-consuming and expensive for institutions.
💡
Limited Scalability
Traditional methods struggle to scale with large student cohorts.
💡
Subjectivity
Human bias can affect the fairness and consistency of moderation.
💡
Delayed Feedback
Slow moderation delays actionable feedback for students and staff.
🎯

Problem Statement

Educational institutions need efficient, objective, and scalable assessment moderation. Current solutions are manual, subjective, and slow—resulting in inconsistent quality and delayed feedback. LOC Analyzer addresses these challenges with AI-powered automation and analytics.

OBJECTIVES

What we set out to achieve

  • 1 Build an AI model for automated assessment moderation
  • 2 Provide real-time analytics dashboards for educators
  • 3 Ensure fairness and consistency in marking
  • 4 Enable scalable moderation for large cohorts
  • 5 Validate effectiveness via pilot testing
